Your Own Case (recommended)

Write down your own task, then work through the five guiding steps to clarify the problem, expose assumptions, and choose a practical next action.

Start here

Describe your own task in the space below. Keep it concrete and personal — this page helps you apply the workshop framework to a real issue you care about.

1. DEFINE

Clarify what success looks like and separate the real goal from the first idea you had.

2. HUNT

List the assumptions you're making about the task, constraints, or outcomes.

3. STRIP

Remove opinions and habits until only the facts you know for sure remain.

4. REBUILD

Use the basic truths to create a better approach, not just a small tweak to the old plan.

5. ACTION

Choose one small next step that you can complete within 24 hours to move forward.

Tip: Answer each step from the same task you wrote above so your next action stays aligned with your real goal.
